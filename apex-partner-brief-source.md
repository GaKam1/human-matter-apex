## What the founders are building

- Grow the Polish business.
- Launch in the United States and United Kingdom.
- Build Human Matter as a global recovery brand.
- Gradually transition Noom in Poland toward Human Matter because of the name collision with noom.com.
- Move the Polish positioning from an orthopedic brand aimed at people aged 60+ toward a recovery brand focused on capability: helping people over 50 remain active and return to activities they value.
- Keep the visual language modern and technology-inspired while focusing the message on mobility, performance and meaningful functional outcomes.

### Brand purpose

Make premium recovery accessible, so health and wellbeing are never defined by status or income.

### Mission

Create high-performance recovery products that combine exceptional quality, thoughtful design, real performance and fair pricing, while building a trusted community that helps people feel better and live more fully.

### Commercial ambition

Reach **$100k days by the end of the year**. This is a founder-stated financial ambition, separate from the purpose of the brand.

**Source:** Kamil's APEX introduction, follow-up team clarification and founder-provided brand statements.
